hour 0-3 notes:
During this period, I made the schematics and most of the pcb. My previous knowledge from the HackPad I did a week ago really helped me make this (although the instructions were much more helpful than that of HackPad's!). I thought I faced problems when I reached the wider keys that don't use a stabilizer when I was arranging them on the pcb, but turns out, the footprint library has them in all the sizes I need and more! This is something that I really believe should be added in the instructions in the future for newcomers.  It also fails to mention that most custom keyboards have the diodes on the backside, and that you have to flip them in the pcb editor. As for the case, I brainstormed some ideas while I was arranging the switches, and came up with an idea to use a Voronoi pattern with cutouts, making it somewhat "see-through"? I'll probably try and improvise when I get there though.
